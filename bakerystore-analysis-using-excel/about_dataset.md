# About Dataset


## Bakery Sales, Customer & Inventory Analytics Dataset

link Kaggle: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/sdcloud/historical-bakery-sales-dataset

### Overview
This dataset is a synthetic bakery retail dataset designed for educational, academic, and machine learning research purposes. It contains 19,321 records and 40 columns representing bakery transactions, customer characteristics, product details, pricing, promotions, environmental conditions, inventory, and business performance.

The dataset covers a two-year period from January 1, 2023, to December 31, 2024, and includes 35 unique bakery products across three stores. It provides a structured foundation for studying bakery sales patterns and developing data-driven decision-support approaches.

### Dataset Contents
The dataset includes the following categories of information:

- **Customer information:** Customer age, gender, customer segment, loyalty membership, and customer ratings.
- **Date and time:** Transaction dates, year, month, day, day of the week, weekend indicator, hour, and time.
- **Product information:** Product name, category, shelf life, storage type, and perishability level.
- **Pricing and sales:** Quantity, unit price, cost price, selling price, discount percentage, and total bill.
- **Promotions and external factors:** Promotion status, promotion type, festival, season, weather, and temperature.
- **Inventory and waste:** Units produced, units sold, unsold units, available stock, and waste percentage.
- **Business performance:** Profit, payment method, and store identification.

### Potential Applications
This dataset can be used for:

- Exploratory data analysis and sales trend visualization.
- Bakery product demand forecasting using machine learning algorithms.
- Product-wise, monthly, seasonal, weekend, and festival sales analysis.
- Inventory planning and stock requirement estimation.
- Unsold stock and potential food-waste analysis.
- Customer segmentation and purchasing behavior analysis.
- Promotion and discount analysis.
- Market basket analysis and product bundle recommendations.
- Sales and inventory dashboards using Power BI or Python.
- Synthetic Data Disclaimer
- This dataset is synthetically generated and is not a verified collection of real bakery business transactions. Customer identifiers and transaction records are included for analytical practice. The patterns and relationships in the data should not be treated as evidence of actual customer behavior or real-world business performance.

### Intended Audience
Students, researchers, data analysts, and machine learning practitioners interested in retail analytics, demand forecasting, inventory management, and recommendation systems.

### Tools
The dataset can be explored using Python, pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, XGBoost, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Google Colab, and Power BI.

### Limitations
The dataset is intended for experimentation and educational use. Model performance and business recommendations derived from it should be validated on suitable real-world data before operational use.
